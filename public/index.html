FAQ

Questions candidates ask us

Is Hirely free for job seekers?
Yes, completely. Creating a profile, uploading CVs and applying to as many jobs as you want costs nothing — now and later.
Who can see my CV?
Only you — and the employers you choose to apply to. Your CV isn't public and isn't searchable; a company sees it only after you send it to one of their jobs.
Can I withdraw an application?
Yes, any time before a final decision. Open "My applications" and click Withdraw. You can even re-apply to the same job later with a different CV.
How will I know if an employer is interested?
You get a notification the moment an employer moves your application — to reviewing, interview, offer or a decision. Each application also shows a progress timeline.
